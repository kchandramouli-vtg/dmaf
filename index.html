    Package with ffmpeg
    
          
    Mixes the stems into a 320 kbps MP3 at their default levels (ffmpeg) and packages them as one IMAF file — an AAC track per stem, with the groups, presets and rule above; MP4Box muxes it when installed. Also computes the waveforms for the asset page's stem visualiser, then pins everything to your IPFS node. Runs on the dev server.

    Publish an MP3
    
    
    Put the audio on IPFS, pin its MPEG-21 MCO agreement next to it, and register both in the DmafMediaRegistry contract through MetaMask. The agreement runs on-chain as an ISO/IEC 21000-23 Smart Contract for Media: every paid play goes through its Contract.payTo, which pays each party its income percentage in the same transaction. Agreements payTo can't settle (parties sharing a wallet, contract wallets, fractional percents) use the registry's direct split instead: it pays each wallet its share itself, in the same transaction.
